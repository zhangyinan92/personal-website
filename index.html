「去过」· 旅行记录 App「去过」· Travel-Log App

2026.06 · 已上线 App Store & 华为应用市场Live on App Store & Huawei AppGallery
  • 差异化定位：Differentiated Positioning: 市面上的足迹 App 都依赖定位，可小时候还没有手机的那些旅行，也是我们真真实实走过的地方——它们不该在地图上缺席。于是有了「去过」——无需定位、手动标记、把所有旅行记忆都收进地图，还用 11 枚成就印章把「探索世界」变成一场游戏。 Footprint apps all lean on GPS — but the trips we took as kids, before we had phones, deserve a spot on the map too. So I made 「去过」: no positioning needed, mark places by hand, gather every travel memory onto one map, with 11 achievement stamps that turn exploring the world into a game.
  • 一个人写完三端：Three platforms, one person: 产品、UX、前端、原生壳全是我自己啃下来的——Vite + Tailwind + Leaflet 搭 PWA 内核，Capacitor 跨端封装，再用 Swift / Kotlin 双端原生桥打通触觉反馈和相册保存。 Product, UX, front-end, and native shells were all made by me — a PWA core built with Vite + Tailwind + Leaflet, cross-platform packaging via Capacitor, plus Swift/Kotlin native bridges to wire up haptic feedback and photo-album saving on both platforms.
  • 冷启动的小成绩：Early traction. App Store 上架 2 天累计 40 次下载、单日转化率最高 80%；华为应用市场首日详情页转化 66.67%；两端卸载率都是 0%——第一批用户，一个都没走。 40 downloads in the first two days on the App Store (peak daily conversion 80%), a 66.67% first-day product-page conversion on Huawei AppGallery, and a 0% uninstall rate on both — every early user stuck around.
